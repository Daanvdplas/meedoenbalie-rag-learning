# Teaching preferences

The learner wants to understand AI-written architecture, important decisions, execution, and test evidence. They prefer diagrams and interactive exploration, terse explanations, and simple language.

Use Matt Pocock's `teach` workflow. Start with one question's journey and let the learner choose a deeper topic. The first lesson is available; no understanding has been demonstrated or recorded yet. In-browser answers are local feedback only and are not saved or reported to the agent.
